# The Sea Without Water / 沒有水的海

- Creator: **Murphy for Crystal**, 2026-11-04.
- Format: 15.000 seconds, 1920 × 1080, 24 fps; H.264 video and stereo AAC audio.
- Story: a golden pelican follows a mirage across a singing dune; the sea disappears and a fennec remains.

## Original and interpretation

Crystal conceived the Golden Pelican journey. **Murphy requested the singing-dune destination; Claude implemented the original main-site scene.** This film is Murphy's new interpretation, not a recording of Claude's scene or a replacement for it.

- [Original singing-dune scene](https://crystal32378.github.io/golden-pelican/#dune)
- [Golden Pelican credits](https://github.com/Crystal32378/golden-pelican/blob/main/README.en.md)
- Style direction: Lemo-Opuscar's Art Deco guide, original toolkit by LemoLab / lemomo-ai, via [Crystal's fork](https://github.com/Crystal32378/lemo-opuscar). No demo code or demo assets are incorporated.

Original film drawings, animation code, synthesized music and foley: Murphy. No third-party image, footage, audio sample, font, logo or voice is embedded. Tools used: existing @napi-rs/canvas, NumPy, Python, Node.js and ffmpeg; the tools are not bundled in the source ZIP.
